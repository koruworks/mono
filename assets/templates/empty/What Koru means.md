# What Koru means

![An unfurling green koru fern frond](koru-fern.jpg)

In Māori, **koru** means *loop* or *coil*. It is the spiral shape of a young silver fern frond as it unfurls toward the light — a symbol of **new beginnings, quiet growth, strength, and renewal**.

> 🌱 The circular coil stays grounded at its center even as it opens outward. That is the shape we wanted for a workspace: calm at the core, expanding cleanly as your ideas grow.

## Why we chose the name

Most tools pull your attention in every direction at once. A real workspace should feel like a clear desk — fast to open, quiet while you think, and structured without getting in your way.

- **Start small, grow naturally** — a single line can become a note, a plan, a checklist, or a whole project.
- **Yours first** — your writing lives on your device, works offline, and belongs to you.
- **Built for clarity** — notes, tasks, files, and AI live together in one calm place instead of scattered across tabs.
